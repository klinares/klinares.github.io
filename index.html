About

I am a survey methodologist and computational social scientist holding an MA in Quantitative Psychology and an MS in Survey and Data Science from the University of Maryland Joint Program in Survey Methodology (JPSM) and the University of Michigan Survey Research Center.

My work sits at the intersection of survey design, psychometric measurement, and applied machine learning within the Total Survey Error framework. I specialize in complex sampling design, variance estimation, item response theory, latent variable modeling, and the responsible integration of AI into survey research pipelines; all built on properly documented and reproducible R, Quarto, and Shiny workflows.

Latent-to-Language: Psychometric Context Engineering for LLM-Based Survey Imputation

IRT · LLM · TSE

Developed the L2L framework — translating IRT/CFA model conclusions into natural-language prompts that guide LLM-based multiple imputation of missing survey items. Compared Claude Sonnet and Llama 4 Maverick against tree-based benchmarks (mixgb, miceRanger) across three psychometric information tiers using 5-fold CV with per-fold model refitting. Evaluated via weighted kappa, latent trait recovery (θ correlation), and Biemer MSE decomposition within a Total Survey Error framework. Uses LAPOP and Latinobarómetro nationally representative survey data.

R IRT / CFA LLM Imputation Multiple Imputation Total Survey Error Quarto

DOGE Days R Over: Reddit Sentiment & AI Stance Classification

Two-paper research pipeline analyzing public sentiment toward federal workforce reductions on Reddit. Paper 1: scraped 557 posts and 12,553 comments across 19 subreddits via the Reddit API, hand-coded 400 comments for stance (54% opposed, 28% favor, 18% neutral), and analyzed post/comment volume spikes around major DOGE news events. Paper 2: applied supervised ML classifiers (SVM, KNN) and deployed LLaMA 3.2B and Gemma 3.12B for zero-shot stance classification, comparing inter-model agreement via Cohen's κ.

R NLP / Sentiment LLM Zero-Shot SVM / KNN Quarto
